# wisp
Introducing the Wind Information Systems Protocol (W.I.S.P.).

Tired of sending boring old messages, completely void of nuance or emotion? Messages that have never let you communicate how you really feel? With our new *text over wind* technology, your words can finally move mountains. Wisp uses real-time geotagged wind data to configure the speed at which your message will send. Giving your words a sweet ol' kiss from Mother Nature herself. Go on, tell them you them. It'll feel like it means something now. Coming soon, to a physical space near you. 

Ephemeral words are sent into the ether on wisps of wind. Carried along to their destination, the message's arrival dictated by a singular force. There exists a longingness that comes with waiting for a message. Adding a hint of humanism to our binary communications through wind and time, we can be more expressive. Wisp rewrites history, attemping to create a timeline where thoughts sent over a network can become more empathetic and sentimental.
